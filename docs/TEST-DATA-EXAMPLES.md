## Example 1: Compliant Murabaha Contract ✅

### Contract Overview
**Contract Type**: Murabaha (Cost-Plus Financing)  
**Asset**: Residential Property at 123 Palm Grove, Dubai  
**Customer**: Ahmed bin Khalid Al-Mansoori  
**Bank**: Emirates Islamic Bank  
**Contract Date**: 15 Rajab 1448 (January 10, 2027)  
**Jurisdiction**: AAOIFI-compliant (UAE)

---

### Full Contract Text

**MURABAHA SALE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on the 15th day of Rajab 1448 (10th January 2027) between:

**SELLER (Bank)**: Emirates Islamic Bank PJSC, a company incorporated under UAE laws, having its registered office at Dubai International Financial Centre ("the Bank")

**PURCHASER (Customer)**: Ahmed bin Khalid Al-Mansoori, Emirates ID No. 784-1985-1234567-8, residing at Villa 45, Jumeirah 1, Dubai ("the Customer")

**WHEREAS**:
1. The Customer has requested the Bank to purchase the Asset (as defined below) and sell it to the Customer on Murabaha basis
2. The Bank has agreed to purchase the Asset and sell it to the Customer subject to the terms and conditions herein

**NOW IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. ASSET DESCRIPTION**
The Asset comprises:
- Property Type: Residential villa (3-bedroom, 2,500 sq ft)
- Location: Plot No. 123, Palm Grove Community, Jumeirah District, Dubai
- Title Deed No.: DLD-2026-789456
- Current Owner: Property Developer LLC (third-party seller)

**2. PURCHASE BY BANK**
2.1 The Bank shall purchase the Asset from Property Developer LLC at the **Purchase Cost** of AED 2,800,000 (Two Million Eight Hundred Thousand Dirhams).

2.2 The Bank's acquisition costs include:
   - Purchase price: AED 2,800,000
   - Transfer fees: AED 56,000 (2% of purchase price)
   - Bank's administrative costs: AED 14,000
   - **Total Bank Cost**: AED 2,870,000

2.3 The Bank shall complete the purchase and take legal ownership of the Asset before offering it for sale to the Customer.

**3. SALE TO CUSTOMER (MURABAHA)**
3.1 Upon completion of the Bank's purchase, the Bank agrees to sell the Asset to the Customer at the **Murabaha Sale Price** of AED 3,440,000 (Three Million Four Hundred Forty Thousand Dirhams).

3.2 The Murabaha Sale Price comprises:
   - Bank's Total Cost: AED 2,870,000
   - Bank's Profit Margin: AED 570,000 (representing approximately 19.9% of cost)
   - **Total Sale Price**: AED 3,440,000

3.3 The profit margin is fixed and disclosed to the Customer as required by AAOIFI Shariah Standard 8.

**4. PAYMENT TERMS**
4.1 **Down Payment**: The Customer shall pay AED 344,000 (10% of Sale Price) upon signing this Agreement.

4.2 **Deferred Payment**: The remaining AED 3,096,000 shall be paid in **120 equal monthly installments** of AED 25,800 each.

4.3 **Payment Schedule**: Installments commence on 1st Sha'ban 1448 (February 10, 2027) and continue monthly on the 1st of each Islamic month for 120 consecutive months.

4.4 **Payment Method**: All payments shall be made via direct debit from Customer's Emirates Islamic Bank account no. 1234567890.

**5. OWNERSHIP TRANSFER**
5.1 Legal ownership of the Asset shall transfer from the Bank to the Customer upon payment of the Down Payment, subject to registration of title deed in Customer's name.

5.2 The Bank shall retain a **security interest** in the Asset until full payment of the Sale Price as permitted under AAOIFI Shariah Standard 8, Section 4.3.

5.3 The Customer shall bear all costs of ownership transfer including registration fees, estimated at AED 68,800 (2% of Sale Price).

**6. EARLY SETTLEMENT**
6.1 The Customer may elect to settle the outstanding balance early at any time without penalty.

6.2 In the event of early settlement, the Customer shall pay:
   - All remaining installment amounts (undiscounted), OR
   - At the Bank's sole discretion, the Bank may offer a **rebate (ibra')** on the outstanding profit portion as a gesture of goodwill, but this is not contractually guaranteed.

6.3 The early settlement amount shall not include any additional fees, charges, or time-based penalties.

**7. DEFAULT AND ACTUAL DAMAGES**
7.1 If the Customer fails to pay any installment within **30 days** of its due date, the Customer shall be deemed in default.

7.2 Upon default, the Customer shall compensate the Bank for **actual damages** incurred, limited to:
   - Administrative costs of collections efforts (not to exceed AED 500 per notice)
   - Legal fees if court proceedings are necessary (evidenced by invoices)

7.3 The Bank shall **NOT** charge:
   - Time-based late payment penalties (e.g., X% per month)
   - Interest on overdue amounts
   - Any penalty exceeding actual documented damages

7.4 The purpose of compensation is cost recovery, not profit generation, in accordance with AAOIFI Shariah Standard 8, Section 5.

**8. INSURANCE**
8.1 The Customer shall maintain comprehensive property insurance covering fire, natural disasters, and third-party liability.

8.2 Insurance must be obtained from a **Takaful operator** (Islamic insurance provider) approved by the UAE Insurance Authority.

8.3 The Bank shall be named as loss payee on the insurance policy until full payment of the Sale Price.

**9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates and interpreted in accordance with **AAOIFI Shariah Standards**.

9.2 Any dispute shall first be referred to the Bank's **Shariah Supervisory Board** for non-binding advisory opinion.

9.3 If unresolved, disputes shall be submitted to arbitration under the Dubai International Arbitration Centre (DIAC) rules, with arbitrators having expertise in Islamic finance.

**10. SHARIAH COMPLIANCE CONFIRMATION**
This Agreement has been reviewed and approved by the Emirates Islamic Bank Shariah Supervisory Board on 10th Rajab 1448. Board members:

### Full Contract Text

**PERSONAL FINANCING AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on March 1, 2027 between:

**LENDER**: QuickCash Financial Services LLC ("the Bank")

**BORROWER**: Rashid Al-Farsi, ID No. 784-1990-9876543-2 ("the Customer")

**1. LOAN AMOUNT**
The Bank agrees to provide the Customer with a **loan** of AED 100,000 (One Hundred Thousand Dirhams) for personal use.

**2. PROFIT RATE**
2.1 The Bank shall charge an **annual profit rate of 8.5%** on the outstanding principal balance.

2.2 The profit rate is subject to review and adjustment **every 6 months** based on market conditions and the Bank's internal benchmark rate (equivalent to EIBOR + 3%).

2.3 If market rates increase, the Bank reserves the right to increase the profit rate by up to 2% per annum with 30 days' notice to the Customer.

**3. REPAYMENT TERMS**
3.1 The Customer shall repay the loan in **48 equal monthly installments** of AED 2,450 each, commencing April 1, 2027.

3.2 Each installment comprises:
   - Principal reduction: Variable (depends on outstanding balance)
   - Profit charge: Calculated monthly as (Outstanding Balance × 8.5% ÷ 12)

3.3 **Total amount repayable**: Approximately AED 117,600 (subject to profit rate adjustments)

**4. LATE PAYMENT PENALTIES**
4.1 If any installment is not paid within 7 days of the due date, the Customer shall pay a **late payment fee of 2% per month** (24% per annum) on the overdue amount.

4.2 The late payment fee is **compounded monthly** if the default continues beyond 30 days.

4.3 Example: If AED 2,450 is overdue, the Customer owes an additional AED 49 after one month, AED 99 after two months (compounded), etc.

**5. EARLY REPAYMENT PENALTY**
5.1 If the Customer repays the loan early, the Customer shall pay an **early termination fee** equal to:
   - 3% of the outstanding principal if repaid in Year 1
   - 2% of the outstanding principal if repaid in Year 2
   - 1% of the outstanding principal if repaid thereafter

5.2 This fee compensates the Bank for lost profit opportunity.

**6. DEFAULT CONSEQUENCES**
6.1 If the Customer fails to pay 2 consecutive installments, the entire outstanding balance (principal + accumulated profit + penalties) becomes **immediately due and payable**.

6.2 The Bank may report the Customer to the UAE Central Bank Credit Bureau, negatively affecting the Customer's credit score.

6.3 The Bank may pursue legal action and charge the Customer for all collection costs, legal fees, and court expenses **plus an additional 5% administrative charge**.

**7. COLLATERAL**
7.1 This is an **unsecured loan**. No specific asset backs the financing.

7.2 However, the Bank reserves the right to demand additional security if the Customer's creditworthiness deteriorates during the loan term.

**8. PURPOSE OF FUNDS**
8.1 The Customer may use the funds for any legal purpose including but not limited to:
   - Personal expenses
   - Business investment
   - Debt consolidation
   - "Other purposes as determined by the Customer"

8.2 The Bank does not require documentation of actual asset purchases.

**9. INSURANCE**
9.1 The Customer is **strongly encouraged** (but not required) to obtain credit life insurance from the Bank's affiliate, CoverAll Insurance LLC, at an additional cost of AED 150/month.

9.2 This insurance covers the loan balance in case of death or permanent disability. Note: CoverAll Insurance is a **conventional insurance provider**, not a Takaful operator.

**10. GOVERNING LAW**
10.1 This Agreement is governed by UAE commercial law.

10.2 Any disputes shall be resolved through UAE civil courts. **No mention of Shariah Supervisory Board or Islamic arbitration.**

**11. SHARIAH COMPLIANCE STATEMENT**
_"This product is designed to comply with Islamic finance principles. The profit rate charged is not interest (riba) but rather a legitimate profit margin on a deferred sale transaction."_

[Note: No actual Shariah Board approval documented, no specific scholars named, no AAOIFI standards referenced]
